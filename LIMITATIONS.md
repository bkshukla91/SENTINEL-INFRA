# Limitations
- Sample data is illustrative, not a live feed.
- Rule-based extraction misses unusual wording; accuracy is untested at scale.
- Network is a simplified hub graph; distances approximate; no load class, bridges, weather, or terrain.
- Scores and speeds are assumptions that a domain expert should calibrate.
- Uses public information only; no classified or restricted sources.
- Coordination: results are decision support, not decisions.
